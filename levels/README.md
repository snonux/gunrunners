# Level format

Levels are plain text, one character per 16x16 px block. Lines starting with
`;` are comments.

`level1.txt` (the training stage) is generated by `tools/mklevel.py`; the
campaign levels `NN_slug.txt` and bonus levels `NN_bonus_slug.txt` by
`tools/levels/NN_*.py`, and the extra levels outside the campaign's
numbering (`extra_slug.txt`, listed in `src/data/campaign.cpp` and played
from EXTRAS on the title screen) by `tools/levels/extra_*.py`; they share
helpers in `tools/levels/lib.py`. Edit
the script and re-run it rather than editing the text file by hand.

A campaign level has a header, a `[map]` and an `[entities]` section:

```
name=STAGE 1 - ROOFTOP RUN
episode=1
theme=neon_rooftops          ; art set
music=theme_synthwave        ; synthesized track id
weapon=pulse_pistol          ; the prototype in the W boxes
par=300                      ; par time in seconds (a tally bonus)
flags=                       ; e.g. clouds (one-way platforms drawn as clouds)
[map]
...one row of characters per block row...
[entities]
@ kind x y key=value ...
```

Bonus levels add `rules=` (`airjump`: jump again in mid-air; `freefall`: no
jumping, you fall and steer until you land on the net, up slows the fall and
down speeds it up; `beatstep`: inputs are queued and the last one runs on the
next beat, a step of 2 blocks or a jump; `sonar`: nothing draws but the
runner, and each shot sends out an echo ring that outlines the walls it
passes; `autorun`: you ride a rubber duck that moves right half a cell a
frame on its own, only jump (6 cells for everyone) and crouch work, and
running into something bumps you back four blocks; `lighttrail`: in the
air your feet leave a one-way neon trail that lasts 45 frames, so a jump
draws a bridge, and holding down drops through it; `flight`: you fly Black
Halo, eight ways at 2 cells a frame with no gravity, fire is its chaingun
and down + fire lobs a rocket; `bounce`: every surface is a trampoline and
you never stand, each landing bounces you back to the last height, 2 cells
higher with jump held, 3 lower with down, 1 lower with neither, 4 to 18
cells, and walls bounce you back; `trapmaster`: you are off the map, up
and down move a cursor along the plates, fire springs the plate's trap and
left and right pan the camera, while treasure hunters walk in from the left;
`negative`: every 75 frames the sun and the moon swap, and with them the
`style=sun` and `style=moon` timer layers, shimmering for the last 15 frames
before they come back; the HUD shows which is up; `pinball`: you are the
ball on a pinball table: hold jump or fire to pull the plunger back and let
go to launch, then jump works the left flipper and fire the right one;
light every lantern to open the gate, the way out, while the drain only
sends you back to the plunger; `floor_lava`: every floor is lava, landing
on an enemy's head bounces you 10 cells up (14 with jump held), lava puts
you back on the last head you bounced on (no damage) and enemies are
harmless; `boulder_surf`: you ride the level's `@ boulder` (`exit=` the
x where the exit ledge starts, `restart=` the block xs a fall goes back
to): left and right speed its roll up or down, you lean the other way on
top and more than 2 blocks off the middle is a fall; jump leaves it, land
back on it or on the exit ledge, anywhere else is a fall; enemies are
harmless and crushed; `golden_touch`: every `@ goldfield` block you stand
on, bump or shoot turns gold with the marked blocks around it, moving
platforms freeze where you touch them, Temple Cats set into 2 x 2 gold
statues, and gold doors stay shut for good if touched or shot before they
open; `recoil_only`: no gravity, no ground, no jump, every shot pushes you
half a cell a frame the other way (8 directions, 2 cells a frame at most,
drag 1/32 a frame), touching an `@ asteroid` or the edge bounces you back
at half speed, and the exit is a return beacon you touch anywhere;
`zero_friction`: every floor is ice with no friction at all, walking adds
1/8 cell a frame up to 1 and only pushing the other way brakes, the runner
stops dead against walls, frozen Puck Drones never thaw and bounce off
walls at full speed, a puck that hits a puck stops and the other goes on,
and you start with the level's prototype, never out of ammo; `grow`: every
gem you pick up makes the runner a quarter bigger, up to twice the size
(their jump grows with them, they can't stand where a taller runner
doesn't fit), every shot shrinks them a step, and from 1.5 x up they break
`by=heavy` blocks by walking into them and small Globs are harmless; `radial_gravity`: each `@ planetoid` pulls the runner toward its centre
within r + 4 blocks, walking follows its surface all the way round, a jump
launches along the surface normal and between the fields the runner drifts
in a straight line (lost for 8 s, it is beamed back to the last planetoid);
`gun_gravity`: down is wherever the runner's last straight shot went (any of
the four walls; walking and jumping follow the current floor);
`stop_motion`: the world (enemies, shots, hazards, pulse rings) moves one
frame only on frames when the runner moves (a direction held, mid-jump or
mid-fall); standing still freezes it all, and the timer counts real time;
`wireframe`: every `#` inside the frame is drawn as a wireframe and is
passable; `@ hitbox rect=` blocks are the only solid ground;
`onehit`: every hit kills, the runner's and theirs;
`split_mirror`: two runners on one input, the top one as usual and the bottom one (`@ twin`) with left and right swapped; both have to stand on their exits at once;
`topdown`: the camera looks straight down: the runner walks eight ways at walk speed with no gravity and no jumping, fire shoots the way it last walked, every `@ building` breaks when walked into (its rating goes on the score), and Model Tanks drive the streets (turning at whatever is in the way, now and then toward the runner) and fire shells in eight directions;
`glide`: no walking or jumping, the runner hangs from a seed head drifting right half a cell a frame; Up and Down steer half a cell a frame, `@ wind dir=u|d push=N` gusts lift or drop it N/2 cells a frame, and thorns (`^`, solid blocks) and `@ dragonfly x y range= speed=` (hovering up and down `range` blocks) bounce it back 4 blocks, no damage; reaching the exit's column ends it;
`turntable`: the room (its solid blocks; `@ grille rect=` blocks let gems out but not the runner) turns 0.4 degrees a frame about its middle while gravity stays down the screen; the runner and the gems slide along whatever is down, and gems out through a grille are lost;
`silent_film`: a silent picture in black and white at 1.5x speed with no sound; each `@ room x text=` puts up a title card (the picture holds 30 frames) when the runner gets there, `@ peel` banana peels slide a runner who moves onto them 4 blocks, a fall of more than 4 blocks ends in a pratfall, a `@ pram x y to=x every=` rolls down the steps and hurts, a `@ laddergag x y h= len=` ladder tips over into a bridge `len` blocks long when climbed to its top, and a shot knocks a Flatfoot's hat off;
`ink`: the Inkwell (`theme=rubber_hose_toon`): hold fire to draw a one-way line from under the runner's leading foot, a cell a frame the way they face (Up or Down held: 45 degrees up or down); the well holds 160 cells (`@ refill x y` adds 80), lines fade after 600 frames, and a runner who falls off the bottom of the page is back at the start with what they collected. Jump, then draw at the top of the jump: a step up;
`wrap_xy`: the Wraparound (Level 29): the map's edges join, left to right and bottom to top; a runner who falls through the bottom comes in at the top with the fall kept, so falling forever is safe. Static Swarms drift across the edges too (a cell every third frame), and a touch puts the runner back where they started, nothing else;
`bubble`: Bubble Up (Level 30, `theme=deep_sea`): the runner rides a bubble that rises half a cell a frame; a tap of jump sinks it two cells and left and right move it half a cell a frame; touching spikes pops it (no heart lost) and puts the runner back at the last `@ ring x y` passed (a row the bubble rose past), or the start;
`bungee`: Bungee (Level 31): the runner is tied to the `@ post x y` by an elastic rope (rest 6 blocks, 14 at most); on the ground, holding a direction stretches it, and letting go flings the runner toward and past the post (up to 4 cells a frame); in the air they swing on it (left and right pump a little) and can fire; each `@ ring x y` flown through scores 200, and the lightning glows but never strikes;
`swap_lr`: Heatstroke (Level 32): every 150 frames left and right swap (the screen wobbles and a swap sign blinks for the 22 frames before); Vulture Drones' scrap knocks the runner back 2 blocks instead of hurting;
`overclock`: Overclock (Level 33): the whole world, the runner included, runs three logic frames for every one; the timer counts real seconds; a Firewall's flames only push the runner back;
`cannonball`: Human Cannonball (Level 34): the runner sits in the `@ cannon x y`; up and down set the angle (30, 45 or 60 degrees) and jump or fire launches at 5 cells a frame; in flight gravity pulls a quarter cell a frame and left and right steer a little; coming down through a `@ net x y x1= score=` scores it, and any landing reloads the cannon after 20 frames;
`copycat`: Copycat (Level 35): every enemy gets the runner's moves each frame, walking at half the runner's speed and jumping when they jump; they never attack, touching one bumps the runner back 2 blocks, and one that falls feet first into a `@ pit rect=` is caught (1000; `goal=collect:N` counts the caught ones);
`throw_all`: Throw Everything (Level 36's bonus): no weapon; fire beside a loose block (`@ loose rect=`, made solid if empty: crates and wall pieces) in the runner's feet row or the two rows above lifts it overhead (8 frames), and so does fire beside an enemy (a `practice_dummy`); fire again throws it 6 blocks ahead (or to the last column open at head height before a wall) in an 8-frame arc, where it lands as a solid, loose block on top of whatever is there; a throw that lands beside a loose stack 1-3 blocks taller lands on top of that instead (stack assist). `@ step x y act=stack arg=COL n=N` tells the bot to stand at (x, y), facing COL, and throw until N blocks stand on COL;
`goal=collect:N` counts the `@ part`s instead of gems),
`timer=` in seconds and `goal=exit`, `goal=collect:N`, `goal=score:N`,
`goal=goals:N` (`zero_friction`: N pucks into the `@ hockeygoal`s) or
`goal=paint:N` (the `goldgate` opens with N % of the marked blocks gold). Files without the
sections (like `level1.txt`) are all map, with an optional `name=` line.

`@` lines place things that need parameters. Positions are blocks (`x y`,
or `x=`/`y=`); `rect=x0,y0,x1,y1` is inclusive; text values with spaces are
quoted; `;` at the start of a line or after a space starts a comment (`path=1,2;3,4` separates points). Kinds so far:

Game logic works in 8 px *cells* like Duke Nukem II, so every block is 2x2
cells. The player is 3 cells wide and 5 tall (4 when crouching, 6 when
hanging), and jumps 6 to 9 cells high depending on the runner.

## Map blocks

| Char | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `#`  | solid block |
| `=`  | one-way platform (jump up through it, land on top) |
| `^`  | spikes: the top half hurts, the bottom half is solid |
| `v`  | spikes hanging from a ceiling: the top half is solid, the bottom half hurts |
| `H`  | ladder: press up to grab it, or down while standing over its top to climb down it; jump off at the top. A climber fills half the block to the ladder's left, so that block is never solid (`.` or a one-way `=`, which a climber passes both ways; `ladders` ctest checks every level) |
| `-`  | hang bar (pipe): you grab it automatically when jumping into it; down aims down, up pulls your legs in, down+jump drops |
| `D`  | force field: solid and deadly until you touch it while carrying the access card |
| `%`  | grate: solid to walk on, sludge rises through it |
| `P`  | player start (feet on the block below) |
| `X`  | exit teleporter (stand on the block below it) |
| `*`  | theme decoration (neon sign, torch, beacon) |
| `B`  | bonus level entrance, 2x3 blocks of TV static (top-left); press up inside it |
| `$`  | hidden gem cache: five gems burst out when you touch it |

## Pickups and actors

Item boxes are 2x2 cells, do not block you, break with one shot (100 points)
and pop out what they hold, Duke style.

| Char | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `g`  | gem, loose (500) |
| `h`  | blue box: health bubble (+1 heart, or 10000 at full health) |
| `m`  | blue box: merchandise (2000, counts for the "all merch" bonus) |
| `r`  | white box: rapid fire for a while (500) |
| `k`  | white box: access card for force fields (500) |

The left, right and top map edges are walls. Dying, or falling off the
bottom, puts you back at the last checkpoint with full health; there are no
lives, as in Duke Nukem II.
