# Gunrunners: what the runners say (docs/VOICES.md has the design).
#
# [level <file>]   a level's lines: `<trigger> | <text>`. Triggers:
#                  start, see <enemy>, at <x> <y>, reach <x> <y> <r>,
#                  time <seconds>, clear. x, y are map blocks.
# [event <name>]   barks on game events: turbo, virus, weapon, letters,
#                  vehicle, respawn, lowhp. Trigger `say`.
# [runner <voice>] catchphrases for the SAY A LINE hotkey, in that voice.
#
# Line ids are the section and the line's number in it (01_rooftop_run_2,
# event_turbo_1, runner_rocco_3); <voice>.txt says where each one is in
# <voice>.ogg. Reordering lines renames their recordings: add new lines at
# the end of a section. tools/voices/ records them.
