Gunrunners is a jump-n-shoot platformer that plays like Duke Nukem II. Pick one of six runners (Dash, Rocco, Nova, Jade, Skye or the robot Bolt) or build your own in the runner editor, switch between them mid-level, and blast your way to the exit.

* A campaign of 42 levels in six episodes, each level with its own twist, its own prototype weapon and its own soundtrack. Playable now: all of Episode 1, Neon Overdrive (Levels 1-7), Levels 8 to 14 of Episode 2, and Levels 15 to 19 of Episode 3; the rest of the 42 levels follow in updates.
* Bonus levels behind flickering TVs, with crazy rules and a timer.
* Secrets: a rubber duck in every level, candid cameras, hidden gem caches.
* An Arsenal of prototypes, Bonus Stars, five savegame slots.
* Smooth HD vector art with glowing neon and parallax backdrops; no pixels.
* Every sound and every note of music is synthesized by the game itself.

Controls:
* Touch: a floating stick on the left, Jump, Fire, Switch runner and Pause on the right. In menus, a d-pad with OK and BACK. Three sizes under TOUCH PAD in the menus.
* Gamepads (Bluetooth or USB): Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro, 8BitDo and most others work out of the box.
* The back key pauses the game.

Gunrunners is free software (GPL-2.0-or-later), with no ads, no tracking and no network access. It also runs on Linux.
