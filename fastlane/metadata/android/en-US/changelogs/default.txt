Jump and fire swapped: Fire is the big corner button, and on a gamepad A fires and X jumps. Touch controls are much easier to hit: the buttons sit at the screen's edges (in the black bars on wide phones), a touch near a button presses it, and a thumb that drifts keeps its button. New: swipe up on the right half to jump, swipe down to switch runner. Gamepads: a pad's Back button now pauses, and the on-screen controls step aside while you use a pad. Releases now also come with a Linux download.
