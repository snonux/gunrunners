New CONTROLS menu: rebind keys and gamepad buttons, resize the touch controls and drag them where you like. Jump and fire swapped: Fire is the big corner button; on a gamepad A fires, X jumps. Touch buttons are easier to hit: they sit at the screen's edges, a touch near one presses it, a drifting thumb keeps its button. Swipe up on the right half to jump, down to switch runner. A pad's Back button pauses, and the touch controls hide while you use a pad. Releases also ship a Linux download.
