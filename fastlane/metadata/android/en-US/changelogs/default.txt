Gunrunners finds its voice. The title screen shouts GUNRUNNERS, every story cutscene is fully voiced (150 lines, 11 voices), and all six runners scream their own lines in levels (1,512 clips) about what they see, Turbo, the Virus, new weapons, G-U-N, vehicles and the last heart. Press V for a line on demand, and set RUNNER VOICES to normal, loud, quiet or off in ASSISTS AND OPTIONS. Custom runners pick a voice in the runner editor.
